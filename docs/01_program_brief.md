# 01. Program Brief: Harbor Assist Launch

> Harbor Docs and Harbor Assist are fictional. This repo shows how I would run a cross-functional GTM launch. All data is simulated.

## Background

Harbor Docs is a document collaboration SaaS company with four plans (Starter, Team, Business, Enterprise) and two routes to market:

- **Self-serve:** customers buy in the product with a card
- **Sales-led:** customers buy through an account executive, with a quote, an order form and an invoice

Harbor Assist is a new AI add-on (summaries, drafting and search across a workspace) priced at **USD 8 per seat per month, billed annually**. Leadership wants it live in both channels on **2 March 2026**.

## Goal

Make Harbor Assist available to every eligible customer in both channels on launch day, with pricing, access and billing working correctly, and confirm within 90 days that customers are buying and using it.

## Scope

| In scope | Out of scope |
|---|---|
| Team, Business and Enterprise plans | Starter plan (not eligible at launch) |
| Self-serve purchase in the product | Free trials of the add-on |
| Sales-led quotes, order forms and invoices | Partner and reseller channel |
| New-term purchases and mid-term additions | Usage-based pricing |
| Access provisioning and entitlement | Changes to core plan pricing |
| Launch reporting and a 90-day readout | |

## Success criteria

Agreed with the VP of Revenue Operations at kickoff. Each one is measured overall and by channel.

| ID | Criterion | Target | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| SC-1 | Attach rate: eligible accounts that buy within 90 days | 8% or higher | Confirms demand in both channels |
| SC-2 | Activation: buying accounts that use the add-on within 14 days of access | 60% or higher | Paid but unused seats are a renewal risk |
| SC-3 | Access: orders with working access within 24 hours | 95% or higher | Customers expect to use what they paid for |
| SC-4 | Billing accuracy: invoices off by more than 0.5% | Under 1% | Billing errors create credits, tickets and churn risk |

## Timeline

| Phase | Dates | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery | 1 to 19 Dec 2025 | Scope and success criteria signed off |
| Requirements | 5 to 23 Jan 2026 | Business requirements approved by each owner |
| Build and configuration | 26 Jan to 13 Feb | All requirements built in their systems |
| UAT | 16 to 25 Feb | All P1 test cases passed |
| Go / no-go | 26 Feb | Readiness checklist complete |
| Launch | 2 Mar | Live in both channels |
| Hypercare | 2 to 13 Mar | Daily issue review, then hand off to owners |
| 90-day readout | 8 Jun | Results against SC-1 to SC-4, with next actions |
